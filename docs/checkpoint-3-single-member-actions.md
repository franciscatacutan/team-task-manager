## Behavior and purpose

- **Single removal:** the dialog uses a bounded header/footer with a scrollable, named and keyboard-focusable details region. Cancel, Close, Escape, backdrop dismissal and duplicate submissions are blocked until the mutation and its existing cache refresh settle. Failures retain the target and reason. It closes only when the result confirms that target's removal without failures; unknown/other-member successes do not close it. Reopening or changing team, target or login creates independent state; late responses cannot close or contaminate the new dialog.
- **Role changes:** the selector has a target-specific accessible name and connected pending/error feedback. The displayed role continues to come from the member list, with no optimistic privilege change. Existing role updates remain serialized within the team/login while saving and refreshing. A failure records the requested role for that member and offers an explicit retry for that exact target/role. A second member's update does not erase the first member's failure. If the refreshed list already confirms the requested role, feedback explains that retry is unnecessary. Disabled targets cannot retry; owner/self/global/team-role/archive restrictions remain in existing permission branches.
- **Organization and local presentation:** `MembersList` delegates editing to `MemberRoleControl`. Both actions reuse existing mutation/cache hooks and the existing fenced submission helper. Role feedback wraps inside its column instead of inheriting table no-wrap styling. The touched editable Member chip and removal button have local contrast corrections; shared badges/palette remain for the bounded contrast inventory.
